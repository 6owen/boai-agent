### iPhone Mirroring Instructions

Available keyboard shortcuts:

- Command-1: Home Screen
- Command-2: App Switcher
- Command-3: Spotlight

Use the `scroll` tool for scrolling, not `drag`.

When clicking apps on the home screen, click the center of the icon instead of the app label.
